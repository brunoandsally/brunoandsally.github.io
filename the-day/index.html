Getting there & getting home

Transport & Parking

⛪

Parking at the church

Very limited near Holy Cross Church. We recommend arriving by taxi, being dropped off, or arranging a lift for the ceremony.

🚗

Parking at Carswell

Guests are welcome to park in the field at Carswell for the reception. Plenty of space — follow the signs.

🚌

Carriages home

Carriages depart Carswell for Plymouth at 11:00pm and 12:30am. Seats limited — the carriage won't wait!

🚕 Recommended taxis

Taxis in this part of Devon fill up fast — especially on September Saturdays. We'll share recommended local firms closer to the day.

⚠ Book early — we cannot stress this enough.

We've pre-allocated on-site accommodation at Carswell for close family — if you haven't heard from us about this, please arrange your own stay. September in South Devon is beautiful, and everything books up quickly!
